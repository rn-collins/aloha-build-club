Why a room beats willpower

This isn't a vibe. Presence and accountability are two of the most reliable behavioral levers in the research literature. We built the club around them on purpose.

+16–32%

The mere presence of another person working nearby measurably raises output — the "peer effect."

Falk & Ichino, Journal of Labor Economics (2006)
76% vs 43%

People who wrote their goals and sent weekly progress to a friend hit 76% of them — versus 43% for those who just kept goals in their head.

Matthews, Dominican University (2015), n=267
~2–3×

A standing accountability appointment — not just intent — is repeatedly linked to multi-fold jumps in follow-through and cooperation.

Fehr & Gächter, American Economic Review (2000)
9M+

Virtual body-doubling sessions have run on Focusmate across 150+ countries — outside evidence that the daily-room model works at scale. It's their number, not ours; we add a build focus and a host on top.

Anything you're making with AI — a product, a content engine, a course, a side project, a job-search sprint. The room and the co-pilot adapt to your project.

Do I need to be technical?

No. If you're using AI tools to build or ship something and you keep stalling, you're exactly who this is for.

How is this different from a course?

It isn't a course. There's nothing to "watch." It's a room you show up to and do the work in, with people and an AI that keep you moving. Information is free now — momentum is the scarce thing.
