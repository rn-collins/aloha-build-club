## 1. The mechanism: presence + accountability changes behavior

**Claim on site:** "The mere presence of another person measurably improves performance."
- **Source (primary):** Falk, A. & Ichino, A. (2006), *Clean Evidence on Peer Effects*, Journal of Labor Economics 24(1), 39–57. Found the presence of a co-worker raised output; effect sizes in the peer-effect literature are commonly cited at **16–32%**. Surfaced via Focusmate's own science page, which cites this paper directly. [JSTOR](https://www.jstor.org/stable/10.1086/497818) · [Focusmate science](https://www.focusmate.com/science/)
- **Historical root:** Triplett, N. (1898) — cyclists rode faster in the presence of others; the founding experiment of *social facilitation*. Michaels et al. (1982) found skilled pool players' accuracy rose ~14% when observed. Use as "psychology's oldest experimental finding," not a precise stat.

**Claim on site:** "Accountability is one of the strongest behavioral levers we have."
- **Source (primary):** Fehr, E. & Gächter, S. (2000), *Cooperation and Punishment in Public Goods Experiments*, American Economic Review 90(4), 980–994. Accountability mechanisms produced **230–310%** improvements in cooperation/contribution. [JSTOR](https://www.jstor.org/stable/117319) · cited by [Focusmate science](https://www.focusmate.com/science/)

**Claim on site:** "Writing your goal down and reporting progress to someone roughly doubles follow-through."
- **Source (primary, peer-reviewed conference):** Matthews, G. (2015), *The Impact of Commitment, Accountability, and Written Goals on Goal Achievement*, Dominican University of California. n=267 adults, 5 groups, 4 weeks. Unwritten-goal group completed **43%** of goals; the group that wrote goals + action commitments + **weekly accountability reports to a friend** completed **76%**. [Dominican Scholar](https://scholar.dominican.edu/psychology-faculty-conference-presentations/3/)

**Claim on site:** "Scheduling a specific appointment to do the work beats a vague intention."
- **Sources (primary):** Locke, E. & Latham, G. (1981), *Goal Setting and Task Performance: 1969–1980*, Psychological Bulletin 90(1) — ~90% of goal-setting studies show specific, challenging goals raise performance. Ariely, D. & Wertenbroch, K. (2002), *Procrastination, Deadlines, and Performance*, Psychological Science — self-imposed deadlines/precommitment improve outcomes. Milkman & Rogers (2015) on implementation intentions / plan-making prompts. [Locke & Latham](http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.90.1.125)

**Claim on site (accountability appointment):** "Committing a goal to another person raises success to ~65%; a standing accountability appointment pushes it to ~95%."
- **Source:** American Society for Training and Development (ASTD) accountability study — widely cited 65% / 95% figures. Treat as a **practitioner statistic**, not peer-reviewed; pair it with Matthews (above) which is the rigorous version of the same finding. [Reference](https://www.afcpe.org/news-and-publications/the-standard/2018-4/the-power-of-accountability/)

## 2. Body doubling specifically (the ADHD-origin practice we're generalizing)

**Claim on site:** "Body doubling — working alongside someone on your own task — helps people start and finish work they'd otherwise avoid."
- **Source (peer-reviewed):** 2024 study in *ACM Transactions on Accessible Computing* found neurodivergent participants use body doubling to initiate, sustain motivation during, and complete tasks.
- **Source (recent, primary):** Lee et al. (2025), *You Are Not Alone: Designing Body Doubling for ADHD in Virtual Reality*, arXiv:2509.12153. Small VR study (n=12): participants finished tasks faster and reported greater sustained attention with a body double vs. alone. **Caveat:** small sample; authors note controlled effects are not yet conclusive. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.12153)
- **Honest framing for the site:** body doubling has strong observational/qualitative support and a solid theoretical basis (social facilitation) but limited large-n controlled trials. Do **not** claim proven causal magnitude. Say "a well-documented practice" — not "clinically proven to increase output X%."

## 3. Proof the model scales (comparable operator)

**Focusmate (closest analog — generic virtual body doubling):**
- **9M+ completed sessions**, thousands of daily users across **150+ countries**. Free tier = 3 sessions/week; Plus = **$8/mo billed yearly / $12/mo monthly**. [Focusmate](https://www.focusmate.com/) · [Pricing](https://www.focusmate.com/pricing/)
- **Self-reported member survey (n=842):** 143% average productivity increase; 91% reported increased performance; neurodivergent members reported 161%. **Label as vendor-reported**, not independent. [Focusmate science](https://www.focusmate.com/science/)

**Sunrise Content Club (closest analog — niche + coaching + pods):**
- Daily live co-working, WhatsApp engagement pod, daily prompt. **$147/mo or $1,500/yr.** Claims 500+ members. Human-coach gated, single time zone, content-only. [sunrisecontentclub.com](https://sunrisecontentclub.com)
- **Gap we exploit:** treats AI as a commodity input, not the product; capped by human staffing; narrow to "posting."

## 4. Market context (why the audience exists and is large)

- **Creator economy:** valued ~**$250B in 2025**, projected ~$310B in 2026 (Grand View / Precedence; estimates vary by firm). [Grand View](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/creator-economy-market-report)
- **Population of the pain:** **200M+ creators** worldwide; **>50% earn under $15k/yr** and ~96% under $100k — a huge base of people trying to build who haven't reached escape velocity, for whom consistency (not talent) is the binding constraint. [inBeat](https://inbeat.agency/blog/creator-economy-statistics)
- **Beyond creators:** the same body-doubling mechanism serves indie builders, founders, students, and job-seekers — a broader TAM than "content creators."

## 5. Claims we will NOT make (integrity guardrails)

- ❌ "Scientifically proven to make you X% more productive." (Overstates the body-doubling evidence base.)
- ❌ Presenting Focusmate's 143% survey as independent fact. (It's vendor self-report.)
- ❌ Income guarantees or "never worry about money again" (Sunrise uses this — it's the weakest, least defensible claim on their page).
- ✅ What we WILL say: presence and accountability are well-evidenced behavioral levers; a daily room + reporting roughly doubles follow-through (Matthews); we sell consistency, not outcomes.
